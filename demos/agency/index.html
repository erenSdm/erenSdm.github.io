[ On principle ]

We don’t do quiet. Loud enough to remember, sharp enough to trust.

VANTA is an independent studio of eleven — strategists, designers, and type engineers working out of one room in Lisbon. We take on eight brands a year, and we finish what we start.

What we do

STR

Brand Strategy

Positioning, naming, verbal identity, and the argument for why you matter.

IDN

Identity Systems

Logotypes, type, and color — plus the rules that keep them alive at scale.

MOT

Motion & Film

Title sequences, brand films, and interfaces that move with real intent.

PRD

Digital Product

Design systems and sites engineered to hold attention and convert it.

EDT

Editorial & Print

Books, packaging, and objects that earn their place on the shelf.

TYP

Sound & Type

Custom typefaces and sonic signatures, drawn and mixed in-house.
